# Cancellation and Refund Policy

## 1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
All digital subscriptions and software licenses come with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are unsatisfied with the product, you may request a 100% refund within 30 days of the initial purchase date.

## 2. Prorated Refunds for Annual Plans
Customers enrolled in annual subscription tiers who wish to cancel after the first 30 days may qualify for a prorated refund for the unused calendar months remaining on their agreement, minus a 10% administrative processing fee. Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing month has begun.

## 3. How to Cancel a Subscription
To cancel your recurring plan:
1. Navigate to **Account Settings** > **Billing & Subscriptions**.
2. Click **Cancel Auto-Renewal**.
3. Confirm your cancellation. You will retain access until the end of your current paid billing period.

## 4. Hardware and Physical Returns
Defective or unsealed hardware items must be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. Returned items must include all original packaging, cords, and documentation. Return shipping postage is prepaid by customer support if the item arrived damaged or non-functional.
