# Account Security and Authentication Guide

## 1. Password Reset Procedures
If you have forgotten your password or your reset link has expired:
1. Go to the login screen and click **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your primary email address. Reset links are valid for 15 minutes for security purposes.
3. If the link expires, request a fresh link. Make sure to check your spam/junk folder.

## 2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Recovery
If you lose access to your authenticator app or your SMS verification code fails to arrive:
- Use one of the 10 emergency backup recovery codes generated during your initial 2FA setup.
- If you do not have backup codes, submit a ticket with identity verification proof (government ID and matching billing receipt) to initiate manual account recovery.

## 3. Suspicious Login Alerts
When an unrecognized device, unusual IP address, or foreign geolocation logs into your profile:
- You will receive an immediate automated alert email with the subject "Security Alert: New Sign-in".
- Click **This wasn't me** in the alert email to instantly terminate all active sessions, invalidate tokens, and force an immediate password rotation.
