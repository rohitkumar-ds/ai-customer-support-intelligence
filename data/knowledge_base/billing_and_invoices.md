# Billing and Invoicing Policies

## 1. Duplicate Charges and Billing Disputes
If your credit card or bank account shows duplicate charges for the same billing cycle:
1. Contact support immediately with your transaction receipt IDs.
2. Duplicate charges caused by payment gateway processing timeouts will be refunded to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

## 2. Tax Exemption and VAT Numbers
Enterprise customers requiring VAT exemptions or specific tax details on invoices can update their profile under **Billing** > **Tax Details**. Provide your valid VAT/GST identification number before the invoice generation date on the 1st of each month.

## 3. Declined Payments and Grace Period
If a subscription renewal payment fails:
- The system automatically retries charging the registered payment method at 3-day intervals (Day 1, Day 4, and Day 7).
- A 7-day grace period is granted before premium features and API keys are temporarily paused.
