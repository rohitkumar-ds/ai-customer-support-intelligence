# Shipping, Tracking, and Delivery Policies

## 1. Tracking Status Updates
Carrier tracking numbers are assigned within 24 hours of order fulfillment. Tracking scans may take up to 48 hours to update once the carrier takes physical possession of the shipment.

## 2. Missing or Stolen Packages
If your tracking status indicates "Delivered" but you have not received your package:
- Check surrounding areas (porch, neighbors, mailroom).
- Wait 24 hours, as some carriers scan packages as delivered prematurely while still in the carrier vehicle.
- If the item remains missing after 24 hours, contact customer support to trigger a carrier trace and initiate a replacement shipment.

## 3. Expedited Shipping Changes
Shipping tiers (Standard, Expedited, Overnight) cannot be changed once an order has progressed to "Fulfillment in Progress". To change shipping methods, you must cancel the order within 60 minutes of placement and submit a new order with the requested delivery speed.
